# Proved case 001 — twice a prime

**Status: PROVED RESULT; elementary and known; novelty not claimed.**

## Statement

For every prime `p`, the even integer `E=2p` has a Goldbach representation.

## Proof

Since `p` is prime by assumption, the two summands `p` and `p` are both prime. Therefore

```
E = 2p = p + p
```

is a valid Goldbach representation. Equal summands are allowed under the project convention.

## Scope

This proves the Strong Goldbach statement only for the subfamily of even integers that are twice a prime. It says nothing about even integers `E` for which `E/2` is composite. No unproved claim is used.

## Significance

This is a baseline restricted case, not a research result and not a route to the full conjecture. It is recorded to keep the proof ledger precise about what is actually proved.
